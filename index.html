Paste your Gemini API key above to turn on AI reviews.

Run an analysis, then press Write AI review for a just-for-fun rating and a written profile with genre weights, moods, likely instruments and listening contexts.
Measurements happen in your browser. Audio is sent only when you ask for an AI review with listening turned on, and then it goes from your browser directly to Google's Gemini API under your own key. Tempo comes from autocorrelating the onset envelope, key from correlating the average chroma vector with Krumhansl–Kessler profiles, and level is unweighted RMS (not LUFS). Character scores and mood are heuristics built from those measurements, so treat them as estimates. Tempo can land on half or double time for some tracks.
